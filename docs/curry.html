Yellow Dal

Ingredients

  • Mustard seeds
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger (small diced piece)
  • Green chili
  • Ground cumin
  • Garam masala
  • Curry powder
  • Ground coriander
  • Turmeric
  • Salt
  • Tomato diced (1 medium)
  • Yellow split peas (1 cup)
  • Chicken-style stock (2.5 cup)

Steps

  • Pressure cooker
  • Stovetop
  1. Cook mustard seeds until sputter. Add onion, garlic, chilli, and ginger. Brown.
  2. Add tomatoes, turmeric, cumin, salt, curry powder, garam masala. Heat through.
  3. Add dal and stock.
  4. Cook 10 mins on high pressure. Naturally release pressure.
  1. Cook mustard seeds until sputter. Add onion, garlic, chilli, and ginger. Brown.
  2. Add tomatoes, turmeric, cumin, salt, curry powder, garam masala. Heat through.
  3. Add dal and stock (use ~3.5 cups stock, as more liquid boils off).
  4. Bring to a boil, then simmer covered ~40–45 mins, stirring occasionally, until the split peas break down. Top up with water if it gets too thick.

Source

  • Adapted from: Urvashi Pitre 2020, Pressure Cooker Rice and Dal | Toor Dal and Rice, https://twosleevers.com/pressure-cooker-rice-and-dal/
  • Inspired by: The Student Kitchen (2016), Herron Books

Brinjal (Eggplant) curry

Ingredients

  • Mustard seeds
  • Brown onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • 1 medium eggplant (cut into chunks and salted)
  • Tinned tomatoes (1 tin)
  • Tomato paste (~2 Tbsp)
  • Chilli
  • Soy sauce
  • Miso paste
  • Coconut cream (50 mL)
  • Vegan beef stock (1/2 cup)
  • Cardamom
  • Paprika
  • Garam masala
  • Cloves
  • Pepper
  • Cayenne pepper
  • Cumin
  • Salt
  • Coriander powder
  • Lemon
  • Sugar (optional)

Steps

  • Pressure cooker
  • Stovetop
  1. Fry mustard seeds in oil. Start to brown onions. Once onions start to brown add eggplant. Once brown add garlic, ginger and chilli and cook quickly. Add spices.
  2. Add in tinned tomatoes and herbs. Add soy sauce, miso paste and coconut milk. Add vegetable or vegan beef stock.
  3. Pressure cook for 20 mins on high pressure. Allow to naturally pressure release.
  1. Fry mustard seeds in oil. Start to brown onions. Once onions start to brown add eggplant. Once brown add garlic, ginger and chilli and cook quickly. Add spices.
  2. Add in tinned tomatoes and herbs. Add soy sauce, miso paste and coconut milk. Add vegetable or vegan beef stock.
  3. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook gently ~40 mins until the eggplant is meltingly soft, stirring occasionally and adding a splash of water if it dries out.

Source

Butter Jackfruit (vegan butter chicken)

Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Margarine
  • Cumin **
  • Paprika ***
  • Cardamom *
  • Curry Powder *
  • Cayenne pepper *
  • Coriander ground *
  • Cloves
  • Fenugreek leaves
  • Turmeric **
  • Garam masala **
  • Cinnamon (stick or ground)
  • Star anise
  • Salt
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Garlic
  • Chilli
  • Jackfruit (1 0.5L tin)
  • 1 cup vegan chicken stock
  • Soy sauce
  • Miso paste
  • Sugar (optional)
  • Lemon
  • Coconut cream (200-400 mL)

Note: * s indicates relative amount (* a little, ** some, *** plenty).

Steps

  • Pressure cooker
  • Stovetop
  1. Add oil and margarine and allow to melt. Once melted, add spices and stir to combine. Then add onion and cook for a while (~10 mins). Add garlic, ginger and chilli for a couple of minutes.
  2. Add jackfruit and coat in spice mix. Then add stock and soy sauce and allow to boil off fluid, stirring occasionally.
  3. Add lemon, stir and taste. If necessary, add sugar. Pressure cook for 10 mins.
  4. Allow to naturally release pressure. Turn on sauté mode and allow additional fluid to cook off so that desired consistency is reached. While this is happening, add coconut cream and extra oil to taste.
  1. Add oil and margarine and allow to melt. Once melted, add spices and stir to combine. Then add onion and cook for a while (~10 mins). Add garlic, ginger and chilli for a couple of minutes.
  2. Add jackfruit and coat in spice mix. Then add stock and soy sauce.
  3. Add lemon, stir and taste. If necessary, add sugar. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook gently ~25–30 mins, stirring occasionally, until the jackfruit is tender and easily pulled apart.
  4. Uncover and let extra fluid cook off until the desired consistency is reached. While this is happening, add coconut cream and extra oil to taste.

Source

  • Inspired by: https://www.reddit.com/r/Cooking/comments/axmknw/ultimate_restaurant_quality_butter_chicken/
  • Inspired by: https://plantbasednews.org/veganrecipes/dinner/butter-jackfruit/
  • Inspired by: https://www.goodto.com/recipes/gordon-ramsay-s-butter-chicken

Notes

  • Other (better?) recipes also use cashews. It might be worth testing these.
  • Can replace jackfruit with tofu.

This sauce is relatively chunky compared to restaurant butter chickens. To overcome this, I think the tomato can be added before the jackfruit and that sauce blended.

Ethiopian bean curry

Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Ginger
  • Chili
  • Garlic
  • Cumin
  • Paprika
  • Cardamom
  • Clove
  • Ground coriander
  • Turmeric
  • Cinnamon
  • All spice
  • Pepper
  • Soy sauce
  • Stock
  • Chopped tomatoes
  • Margarine (2 Tbsp)
  • Red lentils (1/3 cups)
  • Green lentils (1/3 cups)
  • Red kidney beans (x2 cans)
  • Wine/lemon

Steps

  • Pressure cooker
  • Stovetop
  1. Brown onions. Add ginger and chilli and brown. Add garlic and brown.
  2. Add spices and cook until fragrant. Add stock, soy sauce, wine and chopped tomatoes. Add margarine and oil (50 mL) and wait for margarine to melt. Cook until wine starts to boil off. Pressure cook for 20 mins on high.
  3. Add lentils and 2 cups water. Pressure cook for 15 mins on high.
  4. Add beans. Cook on low pressure for 5 mins.
  1. Brown onions. Add ginger and chilli and brown. Add garlic and brown.
  2. Add spices and cook until fragrant. Add stock, soy sauce, wine and chopped tomatoes. Add margarine and oil (50 mL) and wait for margarine to melt. Cook until wine starts to boil off, then simmer covered ~15 mins to build the base.
  3. Add lentils and 2 cups water. Simmer covered ~30–40 mins, stirring occasionally and topping up water, until the lentils are soft.
  4. Add beans and simmer a further ~10 mins until heated through and the stew is thick.

Notes

Replacing the lemon with wine may add more depth.

Misir Wot

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Chili
  • Berbere (~2 Tbsp)
  • Cloves
  • Cardamom
  • Cumin
  • Cinnamon
  • Paprika
  • Nutmeg
  • Coriander
  • Turmeric
  • Tomato paste (~2 Tbsp)
  • Margarine / oil
  • Red lentils (1 cup)
  • Salt
  • Water or vegan stock (~3 cups)

Steps

  • Pressure cooker
  • Stovetop
  1. Dry-fry the onion (no oil) on a low heat until soft and starting to colour, stirring so it doesn’t catch. Add margarine/oil and let it melt in.
  2. Add garlic, ginger and chilli and cook briefly. Add berbere and the rest of the spices and cook until fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste.
  3. Add the rinsed red lentils and stir to coat. Add water/stock and salt.
  4. Pressure cook for 8–10 mins on high, then naturally release.
  1. Dry-fry the onion (no oil) on a low heat until soft and starting to colour, stirring so it doesn’t catch. Add margarine/oil and let it melt in.
  2. Add garlic, ginger and chilli and cook briefly. Add berbere and the rest of the spices and cook until fragrant. Stir in the tomato paste.
  3. Add the rinsed red lentils and stir to coat. Add water/stock and salt.
  4. Simmer covered ~30–40 mins, stirring occasionally and topping up water, until the lentils break down into a thick stew.

Notes

  • Should end up thick and spoonable, not soupy. Cook off extra liquid on sauté mode if needed.
