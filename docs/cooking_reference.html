Cooking info

Quick reference for cooking legumes and grains. Pressure times are for high pressure (the default for most recipes here). Times are starting points — older/drier beans take longer, and different machines vary, so adjust to taste.

Legumes — pressure cooker

Cook on high pressure, then let the pressure release naturally (at least ~10 min) for even, unburst results.

  • Lentils absorb their liquid, so the ratio matters — measure it.
  • Beans are drained after cooking, so exact ratios matter less: just cover them well (roughly 3–4 cups water per cup of beans) and keep below the pot’s max-fill line.
Legume Soak first? Water (per cup) High pressure — dried High pressure — soaked Notes
Red lentils (split) No 1 : 2 8–10 min — Breaks down to a purée — good for dal
Brown lentils No 1 : 1.75–2 8–10 min ~5 min Holds shape reasonably
Green lentils No 1 : 2 10–12 min ~5 min Holds shape
French / Puy lentils No 1 : 2 10–12 min ~5 min Firmest — best where you want them whole
Chickpeas Optional cover well (~1 : 3–4) 35–40 min 10–15 min Soaking greatly cuts time
Red kidney beans Optional cover well (~1 : 3–4) 25–30 min 8–10 min See safety note below
Black beans Optional cover well (~1 : 3–4) 20–25 min 6–8 min
Kidney beans

Dried red kidney beans contain a toxin (phytohaemagglutinin) if undercooked. Cooking them from dry through to fully soft in the pressure cooker destroys it — just don’t stop them short of done, and don’t cook them only in a low-heat slow cooker.

Grains — pressure cooker

Rinse rice and quinoa until the water runs clear first. Cook on high pressure with a ~10 min natural release, then fluff. Keep grains below the pot’s half-full line — they expand.

Grain Water (per cup) High pressure Notes
White rice (long grain / jasmine) 1 : 1 to 1.25 3–4 min
Basmati rice 1 : 1 to 1.25 3–4 min
Brown rice 1 : 1.25 ~22 min
Quinoa 1 : 1.25–1.5 1 min Most of the cook happens during natural release — don’t skip it

Stovetop companion

Approximate simmer times if you’re cooking on the hob instead of under pressure. Use more water than the pressure-cooker ratios (more evaporates), keep a lid on, and top up if it dries out.

Item Water (per cup) Simmer, covered
Red lentils 1 : 3 15–20 min
Brown / green lentils 1 : 3 25–35 min
French / Puy lentils 1 : 3 25–30 min
Chickpeas (soaked) cover well 1–1.5 h
Red kidney beans (soaked) cover well ~45–60 min (boil hard 10 min first)
Black beans (soaked) cover well 1–1.5 h
White / basmati rice 1 : 1.5–1.75 12–15 min, then rest 5 min
Brown rice 1 : 2–2.5 40–45 min, then rest
Quinoa 1 : 1.75–2 ~15 min, then rest 5 min
